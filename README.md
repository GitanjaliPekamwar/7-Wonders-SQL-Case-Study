## 📌 Overview

This project was part of a **Digits and Data SQL Challenge**, where I explored a dataset featuring the **Seven Wonders of the World**.

The challenge aimed to:
- Practice real-world SQL skills 🧠
- Generate actionable insights from structured data 📊
- Boost analytical and storytelling capabilities ✍️

From Asia to South America, I queried my way through some of the world's most iconic landmarks using **SQL**, diving into tourism, reviews, ratings, and geography.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset & Schema

The dataset consists of **multiple interrelated tables** containing:
- Wonder names
- Locations
- Visitor statistics (2021–2023)
- Ratings & reviews
- Categories & continents

The schema was simple yet powerful—designed to test various SQL concepts like aggregation, filtering, joins, and string functions.

---

## 🧠 Analytical Questions Explored

Here are some of the engaging questions I answered with SQL:

1. 📍 **Which wonders exist and where are they located?**
2. 👥 **Total visitors per wonder in 2023 (with a twist: `CONCAT` used!)**
3. 🧱 **Visitor insights for the Great Wall of China**
4. ✍️ **Total number of reviews submitted**
5. 🌎 **Map wonders to their respective continents**
6. 📈 **Average annual visitors from 2021–2023**
7. ⭐ **Average rating of each wonder**
8. 🔍 **Wonders with 1–3 million visitors in 2023**
9. 💬 **Find all reviews that mention ‘beautiful’**
10. 🧭 **Wonders located in South America or Asia**
